Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our event bookings, delivery areas, and masterclass schedules.

How far in advance should I book private event catering?

We recommend booking at least 3 to 4 weeks prior to your event date to ensure barista and equipment availability, especially during peak weekend seasons.

Are mobile espresso bar setups included with catering?

Yes! All catering packages include our modular wooden espresso bar, professional dual-boiler machines, grinder, cups, syrups, and professional baristas.

Can corporate subscriptions be customized for employee preferences?

Absolutely. We offer a mix of light, medium, and dark roasts, as well as decaf options, tailored specifically to your office coffee break requirements.
