The Roasting Journey

Follow raw green coffee beans through our precision roastery stages.

01

Green Grading

Hand-sorting green beans to screen out defects and guarantee consistent moisture levels.

02

Drum Charging

Introducing beans into our cast-iron roastery drum pre-heated to precise thermal targets.

03

First Crack

Monitoring acoustic expansion and exothermic reactions as sugars begin caramelizing.

04

Cooling Tray

Rapid air cooling to halt the roasting reaction instantly and lock in delicate flavor profiles.
