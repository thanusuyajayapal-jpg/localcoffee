Quick Support FAQ

Do you take table reservations?

We operate on a first-come, first-served walk-in basis for daily café seating, but large groups can contact us ahead.

Is parking available onsite?

Yes, free 2-hour visitor parking is available directly behind our building along Artisan Lane.
